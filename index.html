Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Get detailed clarity on UGC rules, exam process, EMI options & job validity.

Are online degrees fully valid for Government Jobs & Higher Studies in India? +
Yes, absolutely. As per the official UGC (Universities Grant Commission) notification, online degrees awarded by UGC-DEB approved universities are treated at par with traditional on-campus regular degrees for government job examinations (UPSC, Banking, SSC), private jobs, and higher studies in India or abroad.
How are semester examinations conducted in online courses? +
Exams are conducted digitally through AI-proctored online test software. You can safely give exams from your home/office using a standard web browser and webcam. Some universities also give flexible time slots on weekends to fit working professionals' routines.
Can I pay my course fees in monthly EMI instalments? +
Yes, most top online universities provide easy 0% interest monthly EMI options starting as low as ₹5,000/month. You can choose to pay per semester or through monthly banking EMI arrangements.
Do online universities provide campus placements or career assistance? +
Yes! Universities organize virtual placement drives, resume building workshops, mock interview sessions, and job fair portals with 300+ hiring partners like TCS, Infosys, Wipro, ICICI Bank, and Amazon.
What is the eligibility criteria for Online MBA and MCA admission? +
For Online MBA, a Bachelor's degree (any stream) with a minimum 40%-50% aggregate score is required. For Online MCA, a graduation degree (BCA, B.Sc, B.Com, or B.A.) with Mathematics at 10+2 or Graduation level is generally required.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Got questions? We have answers.

How do I verify if an online university is UGC-DEB approved? +
You can check the official UGC-DEB web portal or review the accreditation badges on Eduvanta. All listed universities on our platform are verified with active UGC-DEB entitlements.
Is exam attendance required on-campus or online? +
Most modern online degree programs conduct online proctored exams. You can take exams safely from your home using a web camera and laptop.
Can I pay course fees in easy monthly EMI installments? +
Yes! Universities provide 0% interest EMI options through banking partners. Monthly EMI rates typically range between ₹5,000 to ₹9,000 per month.
How do I receive the university brochure & syllabus PDF? +
Click on "Full Profile" for any university, select your course, and click "Download Brochure". Fill out the short inquiry form to receive the PDF immediately.
